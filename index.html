AI-Powered Venture Ecosystem

Venturo AI 是 AI 驱动的创投生态， 以创业者为核心，连接资本、专家与产业伙伴， 帮助创业者发现机会、建立连接并持续获得反馈。

Venturo AI is an AI-powered venture ecosystem centered on entrepreneurs, connecting them with investors, experts and industry partners to discover opportunities, build meaningful connections and receive continuous feedback.

我们相信，创业生态中的信息、资源与人与人之间的连接， 可以通过 AI 被更有效地组织起来。 从 AI 情报、用户画像与兴趣，到项目发现、智能匹配与连接， Venturo AI 正在构建一套持续学习的创投基础设施。

We believe information, resources and human connections across the venture ecosystem can be organized more effectively with AI. From intelligence and user interests to project discovery, intelligent matching and connections, Venturo AI is building a continuously learning infrastructure for venture.

当前 V1.0 聚焦创业者、投资人、专家/顾问与企业/产业合作方， 通过真实行为与反馈不断完善用户画像与匹配能力。

V1.0 currently connects entrepreneurs, investors, experts and industry partners, continuously improving user profiles and matching capabilities through real-world behavior and feedback.
